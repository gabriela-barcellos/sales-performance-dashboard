# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

End-to-end data analytics project developed using **SQL** and **Power BI** to analyze sales performance from the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset**.

This interactive dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders monitor revenue, sales performance, customer behavior, seller activity, and product category performance.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

E-commerce companies generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Without an effective reporting solution, it becomes difficult to monitor business performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

This project aims to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders to track key business metrics, analyze revenue trends, evaluate order fulfillment, and identify top-performing product categories.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following business metrics and visualizations:

- Total Revenue
- Order Count
- Delivered Orders
- Total Sellers
- Average Review Score
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Order Status Distribution
- Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue
- Customer State Filter

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Health & Beauty** generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Approximately **97% of orders** were successfully delivered, demonstrating a strong fulfillment rate.
- Monthly revenue showed a consistent upward trend, reaching its highest levels between late **2017** and early **2018**.
- The average customer review score was **4.09**, indicating a high level of customer satisfaction.
- The average order value was approximately **R$154.10**, providing a useful benchmark for sales performance.
